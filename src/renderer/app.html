    Warnings

    The most recent posture warnings in this period

    1. Allow camera access

    Posture Guard opens your camera for about 2 seconds at each check, measures your posture on this computer, and closes the camera again. No images are saved or sent anywhere.

    2. Calibrate your good posture

    Starting camera…
    1. Place the camera at or slightly below eye level, so your head and both shoulders are in view.
    2. Sit the way you want to sit: back against the chair, shoulders relaxed, ears over shoulders, screen at arm's length.
    3. Click Calibrate and hold still for 3 seconds.

    3. Settings

    Changes save automatically. Closing this window keeps Posture Guard running in the system tray; use Quit in the tray menu to stop it.
